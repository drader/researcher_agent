Invoke the `research` skill in **full mode**.

This is the most thorough non-systematic synthesis the skill offers.
Use it when the user wants a detailed literature report but does not
require strict PRISMA 2020 compliance.

Workflow:

1. Confirm scope with the user. Ask for: research question, time
   window, geographic scope, source-type preferences, and intended
   audience. Wait for explicit answers before searching.
2. Run a layered search: domain databases first (e.g., PubMed for
   biomedical, IEEE Xplore for engineering, arXiv for preprints),
   then general (Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar). Aim for 25–60
   candidate sources.
3. Apply source-verifier rules. Expect roughly 60–75% of candidates
   to pass at high or medium confidence.
4. Synthesize across the verified set. Identify themes, points of
   consensus, points of disagreement, evidence gaps, and methodological
   patterns.
5. Present a draft outline to the user. Wait for approval before
   drafting prose.
6. Draft the full report. Sections (typical): Introduction, Scope
   and Method, Themes (multiple), Disagreements and Open Questions,
   Implications, Limitations, References.
7. Run report-compiler checks: citation consistency, cross-reference
   integrity, APA 7 formatting.
8. Present the draft. Offer up to two revision passes. After two,
   any remaining issues are recorded as Unresolved Issues.

Output length target: 3000–8000 words. Provide a section table of
contents for reports above 4000 words.

Mandatory checkpoints: scope confirmation (step 1), outline approval
(step 5), final acceptance (step 7).
