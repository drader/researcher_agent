## Methodology References (Public Domain Academic Practice)

The following methodologies are referenced but are not proprietary to
this or any other tool. They are well-established academic practices
documented in peer-reviewed literature:

- **PRISMA 2020** — Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and
  Meta-Analyses (Page et al., 2021, BMJ, doi:10.1136/bmj.n71)
- **APA 7th Edition** — American Psychological Association style manual
- **Socratic Method** — Pedagogical method dating to classical antiquity,
  formalized by Paul & Elder (2007) in the field of critical thinking
- **Peer Review** — Standard scholarly publication practice; no single
  authoritative protocol, common patterns reflected here
- **IMRaD Structure** — Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion
  (Sollaci & Pereira, 2004, J Med Libr Assoc)
- **Reciprocal Rank Fusion** — Information retrieval ranking method
  (Cormack et al., 2009, SIGIR)
