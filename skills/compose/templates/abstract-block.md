## Abstract — structured variant

<!-- Use when the venue requires structured headings. Common headings:
     Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion. Some venues use
     Objective, Design, Setting, Participants, Interventions, Main
     outcomes, Results, Conclusions. Use the venue's required
     headings. -->

**Background.** {{One to two sentences naming the problem the study addresses, the gap in current knowledge, and why the question matters. No citations.}}

**Methods.** {{Two to three sentences describing the design, the data or sample, the procedure, and the analysis. Tense: past. Compressed: enough that the reader can judge the study's basis without consulting the body.}}

**Results.** {{Three to four sentences reporting the principal findings. Preserve key numbers (sample sizes, effect sizes, confidence intervals or equivalents). Do not introduce results not reported in the body.}}

**Conclusion.** {{One to two sentences naming what the work contributes and to whom. Do not over-claim. The conclusion in the abstract should match the conclusion in the body.}}

## Abstract — unstructured variant

<!-- Use when the venue does not require headings. The same four
     moves still appear in order; they are not labeled. -->

{{Single paragraph, typically 150–250 words. Sentence 1: the problem. Sentences 2–3: the approach. Sentences 4–6: the principal findings with key numbers. Sentence 7–8: the contribution. The paragraph should read as one piece, with each sentence flowing into the next.}}
