## Notes for the drafter

- Every citation must appear in the manuscript's reference list.
- Avoid stacking citations as decoration (`(Smith, 2020; Jones, 2021; Brown, 2022)` with no engagement with what each work argued).
- Where two cited works disagree, name the disagreement; do not paper over it.
- Where a citation supports only part of a claim, cite only for that part.
- The section should not exceed the venue's expected length for the role it plays (Introduction middle vs. dedicated Related Work).
